# Contributing

Corrections and evidence-backed additions are welcome.

## Before proposing a tool

Please confirm that the tool:

- Solves a clear, practical job;
- Has an official product page;
- Has accessible pricing or plan information;
- Has an official privacy, security, or data-handling source; and
- Is not being submitted as undisclosed advertising.

## What to include

- Tool name and official URL
- Specific job-to-be-done
- Free-access status with an official source
- Privacy or operational checkpoint with an official source
- “Avoid if” condition
- Review date
- Disclosure of any employment, investment, affiliate, or sponsorship relationship

Do not label an entry “hands-on tested” without the evidence required by [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md).

## Corrections

For broken links, changed pricing, acquisitions, shutdowns, or policy changes, open an issue with the current official source. Clear corrections are prioritized over new listings.

## Sensitive information

Never submit API keys, private customer data, private meeting recordings, confidential source material, or personal information that you are not authorized to publish.

## Editorial decisions

Being useful is necessary but not sufficient for inclusion. The directory stays intentionally small, and similar tools may be declined until a meaningful outcome or constraint differentiates them.
